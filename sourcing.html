Where our cloth comes from

We buy from five textile hubs, each known for something different.

Surat, Gujarat

Synthetics and embroidery

India's biggest synthetic fabric market. We buy georgette, chiffon, crepe and embroidered suit material here, plus work sarees at mill prices.

Kolkata, West Bengal

Cotton and handloom

Soft cottons, Tant and Baluchari sarees, Kantha stitch work and a wide choice of readymade shirts from the Burrabazar wholesale lanes.

Jaipur, Rajasthan

Block print and bandhej

Hand block printed cottons from Sanganer and Bagru, bandhej (tie-dye) dupattas and sarees, and light hand-dyed suit fabrics.

Delhi

Readymade and wholesale

Our source for readymade ladies' suits, kurta sets and festive wear. Delhi's wholesale markets set the trend for each season.

Banaras, Uttar Pradesh

Silk and brocade

Banarasi katan silk, kora, tanchoi and brocade for weddings and festivals, woven by weaver families in and around the city.
